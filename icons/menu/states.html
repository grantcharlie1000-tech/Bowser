Bowser icon states

Each row uses inline SVG on a 24-unit grid. Hover lifts the glyph, press gives instant 0.96 scale, and active uses the accent with a short key-stroke draw. Reload and reset turn on press; back and forward nudge in their direction. The links pin a single resolved state for screenshots.
