Bowser rail icon states

Every rail icon and workspace glyph inline on its 24-unit grid, in the four states the Rust rail paints. Idle is the resting stroke. Hover lifts the icon to 1.06 and thickens the pen by 13% (ease-out, 150 ms). Pressed lands at 0.96 on the same frame, taking over from the hover lift. Active brightens the ink, draws an accent trace along the strokes in path order (180 ms ease-out) and grows the accent bar in. Releasing reverses each over 120–140 ms ease-in. With prefers-reduced-motion every state snaps with no transition.
